    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Foodlet is the first interface I ever built. It's a third as polished as
        what I make now, and it still holds a place in this portfolio, because
        everything started here. It began with a fairly casual observation in
        class: middle-class restaurants in Taiwan had already taken over mobile
        payment, and it actually happens in the moment of pulling out your phone
        and walking out the door — a beep, and that's it. Probably the least
        memorable second of the whole meal. So I wanted to know: if a wallet was
        designed by someone who cared about the meal instead of the transaction,
        what would it look like?
