      - en: >-
          Foodlet is the first interface I ever built. It's about a third as
          polished as my current work, but everything started here, so it stays
          in the portfolio. The idea came from a casual observation in class:
          mid-range restaurants in Taiwan had almost all adopted mobile payment,
          and paying took only the few seconds between taking out a phone and
          walking out the door. One beep and it was over, probably the least
          memorable moment of the meal. I wanted to know what a wallet would
          look like if it were designed by someone who cared about the meal
          itself.
